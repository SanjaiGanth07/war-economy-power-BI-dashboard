# 🌍 Global Economy — War Impact Analysis (2022–2026)

## 📌 Overview

A Power BI dashboard analyzing how the **Russia-Ukraine War (2022)** and **Iran-US War (2026)** impacted global energy prices, India's LPG costs, and the broader economy.

> *"How does a war thousands of miles away impact an Indian household?"*

---

## 📄 Dashboard Pages

| Page | Topic | Key Visual |
|---|---|---|
| Page 1 | Global Oil & War Impact | Brent Oil Line Chart 2020–2026 |
| Page 2 | India LPG Crisis | City-wise LPG Comparison |
| Page 3 | GDP & CPI Impact | Waterfall & Area Charts |

---

## 💡 Key Findings

| Indicator | Before War | After War | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brent Oil (USD) | $88 | $118 | 📈 +34% |
| Chennai LPG (₹) | ₹905 | ₹928.50 | 📈 +₹24 |
| Global GDP% | 3.3% | 3.1% | 📉 -0.2% |
| Global CPI% | 3.8% | 4.4% | 📈 +0.6% |
| India CPI% | 4.8% | 3.21% | 📉 -1.59% ✅ |

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Data Sources

**Tools:** Power BI Desktop | Microsoft Excel | Power Query

**Data:** IMF | World Bank | FAO | WEF | PIIE

---

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Download **War_Economy_Dashboard.pbix**
2. Open with [Power BI Desktop](https://powerbi.microsoft.com/desktop) (free)
3. Use slicers to filter by war period
4. Compare Before vs After war values
